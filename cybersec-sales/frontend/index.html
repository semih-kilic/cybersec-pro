Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is CyberSec Pro? ▼
CyberSec Pro is a cloud-based security platform that gives you access to all 682 Kali Linux tools through your web browser. No installation, no VM setup - just instant access to professional penetration testing capabilities.
Is this legal to use? ▼
Yes, but only for authorized testing. You must have explicit written permission to test any system you don't own. Unauthorized testing is illegal.
How does the free trial work? ▼
Sign up with just your email - no credit card required. You get 14 days of access to 20 tools with 10 scans per day. After the trial, you can upgrade to a paid plan.
Are the tools always up to date? ▼
Yes! We sync with Kali Linux repositories daily and automatically update all tools. You always have access to the latest versions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need before you buy

What are the system requirements? ▼
Recommended: Kali Linux (2023+), Ubuntu 20.04+, or Debian 11+
Hardware: 4GB RAM minimum (8GB+ recommended), 10GB disk space
Permissions: Some tools require sudo/root privileges
Alternative: Docker on Windows/macOS/Linux
Browser: Chrome, Firefox, or Edge (latest)
What do I actually get when I buy? ▼
You get the CyberSec Pro dashboard, integrations, installers, and updates. The security tools themselves are open-source and licensed separately. We provide the interface and presets; you may need to install missing tools depending on your OS.
Do I need to install the tools separately? ▼
On Kali Linux, most tools are pre-installed. On other distros, many tools require installation. The dashboard shows which tools are missing and provides install guidance.
Why do the tool counts look different? ▼
The total catalog is ~230 tools, but the installed number depends on your OS and what you’ve installed. Counts are approximate and will vary by distribution and setup.
Does it work on Windows or macOS? ▼
Not natively. Use Docker (recommended), a Linux VM, or WSL2 on Windows.
Is internet required? ▼
Internet is required for downloads, updates, license activation, and scanning remote targets. The app runs locally on your machine.
Where is my data stored? ▼
Scan results and reports are stored locally on your machine. We don’t require uploading scan data to our servers.
Can I run scans on any website? ▼
Only test systems you own or have explicit written permission to test. Unauthorized security testing is illegal in most countries. Use it for your own assets, authorized engagements, or labs.
Do you guarantee results? ▼
No. Results depend on your targets, permissions, tool configuration, and your own testing skill. CyberSec Pro is a professional toolkit, not a “one‑click hack.”
What kind of support do you provide? ▼
Basic: Email support (48–72h)
Professional: Priority email (24h)
Enterprise: 24/7 with dedicated contact

We support installation, the dashboard, and integrations. We don’t provide support for third‑party tool bugs or misuse.
Is there a refund policy? ▼
We provide a 14‑day free trial. Because this is a digital product, we do not offer refunds after purchase. If you have technical issues, contact support.
How do I activate my license after purchase? ▼
1) You receive a license key by email
2) Download from this website
3) Open Settings → License
4) Paste your key and activate
Can I use one license on multiple computers? ▼
Basic and Professional are single user, single machine. For multiple installations, choose Enterprise or contact us for volume licensing.
How often do you release updates? ▼
We ship regular updates for the dashboard, installers, and presets. Tool updates depend on upstream open-source projects and your OS packages.
How fast can I get onboarded? ▼
Most users are up and running in 10–15 minutes using the one‑line installer. Enterprise onboarding includes guided setup and rollout planning.
Can I transfer my license to a new machine? ▼
Yes. If you replace your machine, contact support to transfer your license.
Does the Docker option include all tools? ▼
The Docker option provides a Kali-based environment with broad tool coverage. Exact tool availability can vary by image and updates.
Can I use it offline? ▼
Most features work offline once installed. Internet is needed for activation, updates, and scanning remote targets.
Where are reports stored? ▼
Reports are saved locally on your machine. You control where to export or share them.
Is it safe to install? ▼
CyberSec Pro runs locally and does not require sending your scan data to us. Install only on machines you trust and keep your system updated.
Are updates included? ▼
Yes. License holders receive dashboard updates. Tool updates depend on upstream open-source projects and your OS package manager.
What are the trial limits? ▼
The trial is time-limited (14 days). Some features and tool access are restricted; details are listed in the download section.
How does the trial license activate? ▼
The 14‑day trial activates automatically on first run and requires internet access.
When will I receive my license key? ▼
Your license key is emailed after payment completes. If you don’t see it within a few minutes, check spam/junk or contact support.
How do I verify downloads are safe? ▼
Download only from semihkilic.com over HTTPS and verify checksums using version.json. If a checksum doesn’t match, do not run the file.
Can the trial be hacked? ▼
No software can guarantee zero risk. The app runs locally and does not expose remote access by default. Keep your OS updated, use strong credentials, and only test authorized targets.
Do you offer team or multi-user licensing? ▼
Yes. Enterprise includes multi-user licensing and support options. Contact sales for team pricing and deployment guidance.

Our Mission

Democratize Security Testing

The average cost of a data breach reached $4.88 million in 2024 — yet most small and mid-sized organisations still lack access to the same security testing tools available to Fortune 500 companies. We're changing that.

Accessibility

Enterprise-grade security tools should not require a six-figure budget. We package 396 tools into plans that start free — no VM setup, no CLI expertise needed.

Transparency

Every scan produces detailed, reproducible output. Raw tool results alongside parsed findings — so your team can verify and learn, not just tick boxes.

Privacy & Trust

Your scan data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). Multi-tenant isolation ensures no organisation ever sees another's results.

Continuous Protection

Scheduled scans, 24/7 monitoring, and real-time WebSocket alerts mean vulnerabilities are caught the moment they appear — not months later.

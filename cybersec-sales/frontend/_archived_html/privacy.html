Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026. How we handle your data with full transparency.

Introduction

CyberSec Pro ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our cloud-based penetration testing platform.

By using CyberSec Pro, you consent to the data practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our services.

Information We Collect

Account Information

When you register, we collect your name, email address, and organization details. If you use GitHub OAuth, we receive your public profile information.

Usage Data

We automatically collect information about how you use the platform:

  • Scan targets, configurations, and results
  • Tool usage patterns and frequency
  • Report generation history
  • Login timestamps and IP addresses
  • Browser type and device information

Payment Information

Payment processing is handled by Stripe. We do not store credit card numbers. Stripe's privacy policy applies to payment data.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the penetration testing platform
  • To process your subscription payments
  • To send security alerts and scan notifications
  • To generate compliance reports
  • To improve our tools and user experience
  • To detect and prevent abuse of the platform

Data Storage & Security

All data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). Scan results and reports are stored encrypted in our PostgreSQL databases. We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • End-to-end encryption for all API communications
  • SOC 2 compliant infrastructure
  • Regular penetration testing of our own platform
  • Role-based access control (RBAC)
  • Multi-factor authentication support

Data Retention

We retain your data for the duration of your active subscription. Scan results and reports are retained for 12 months after generation. Upon account deletion, all personal data is purged within 30 days.

Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Rectify inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Object to data processing
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@cyber-sec-pro.com.

Third-Party Services

  • Stripe — Payment processing
  • GitHub — OAuth authentication
  • Redis — Session management (self-hosted)

Cookies

We use essential cookies for authentication (JWT tokens) and session management. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising cookies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Significant changes will be communicated via email or platform notification.

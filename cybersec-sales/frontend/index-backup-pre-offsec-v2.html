FAQ

Questions & answers

What exactly is CyberSec Pro?

CyberSec Pro is a cloud-based platform that provides browser access to 401 Kali Linux security tools across 61 categories. You can run penetration tests, vulnerability scans, and security assessments without installing anything locally.

What does the free trial include?

14 days of platform access with 50 security tools. You can run one comprehensive scan and generate a detailed PDF report. No credit card is required to start. Upgrade anytime for more tools.

Is penetration testing legal?

Yes, when performed on systems you own or have explicit written authorization to test. CyberSec Pro is intended for authorized security testing only. You are responsible for ensuring you have proper authorization.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes. All plans are month-to-month. Cancel anytime through your dashboard settings. You keep access until the end of your billing period.

Do you have an API?

Professional and Enterprise plans include full RESTful API access. You can integrate scans into CI/CD pipelines and automate security testing. See our documentation.

How do I get support?

Email us at cybersecpro@semihkilic.com. Free and Starter plans get email support. Professional gets priority support. Enterprise gets a dedicated contact.

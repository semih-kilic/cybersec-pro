Security FAQ

Can other users see my scan results?

No. All user data is completely isolated. Each session runs in a separate container with no shared resources.

How long is my data stored?

Active account data is retained while your account exists. Deleted accounts and their data are purged within 30 days.

Do you log my tool usage?

We log basic activity for security and debugging. Command output is not stored long-term unless you save it to a project.

Is my connection to targets monitored?

We do not inspect the content of your scans. However, we reserve the right to terminate accounts engaged in illegal activity.
